Thank you for purchasing our website template. We are committed to providing helpful support to help you configure and deploy your new luxury real estate portal successfully.

## How to Get Support

1. **Marketplace Profile**: Visit our profile page on the marketplace where you purchased this template and click the "Support" tab or "Send Message" button.
2. **Email Support**: Reach out directly via email at `support@veridianestates.com` with your **Purchase Code** and details of your request.
3. **Response Window**: We respond to all queries within **24–48 hours** (Monday through Friday, excluding major public holidays).

## Support Policy Coverage

### What We Cover:
* Fixing bugs or code errors found in the original template files.
* Answering general questions about setup and file structure.
* Standard features configuration questions.

### What We DO NOT Cover:
* Third-party integrations (e.g., setting up active databases, CRM backends, or custom API endpoints).
* General hosting/server configuration problems.
* Custom feature requests or major design rewrites.
* Basic HTML/CSS/JavaScript training.

## Quick FAQ

### Q: How do I change the default brand colors?
**A:** Open `Template/css/variables.css` and locate the `:root` variables block. Update the values of `--primary`, `--primary-rgb`, `--accent`, `--accent-rgb`, and other color tokens. The entire site layout, hover states, and overlays will automatically adapt to your new color scheme.

### Q: Why isn't my contact form sending emails?
**A:** This is a static frontend website template. The forms use simulated server actions via JavaScript mock timeouts. To send actual emails, you must connect the form's action attribute to a server script (such as PHP `mail()`, Node mailers) or an online form collection service (e.g., Formspree, Web3Forms, Netlify Forms).

### Q: Are the demo photos included in the purchase?
**A:** Yes, the packaged WebP/PNG images are generated for demo placeholders and are licensed under Creative Commons Zero (CC0) or generated via AI. You are free to use them or replace them with your own listings images under `Template/assets/images/`.
